Source: provided project `korean-learning-app/DESIGN.md` and bundled Pretendard font.

```css
--bg: oklch(98% .008 90);
--surface: oklch(100% 0 0);
--fg: oklch(20% .012 75);
--muted: oklch(48% .012 75);
```

- Display and body: Pretendard Variable, with a system sans fallback.
- Warm editorial canvas with restrained hairlines and elevated white surfaces.
- Korean content is visually primary; English is progressive assistance.
- Muted persimmon is reserved for selected states and primary actions.
- Media and transcript form the core workspace; dashboards stay secondary.
